This Special Skill ties the behavior of entire armies to the user's discipline and state of mind.

Update PDF 5.2, October 2025

INSPIRING LEADERSHIP
AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory
EFFECTS
  • If the user belongs to a Fireteam where all members have the Regular Training Characteristic listed in their Unit Profiles, apply a +2 inch bonus to the Team Leader’s Zone of Control when making Coherency Checks for the Fireteam.
  • Restriction: This bonus is not cumulative with any bonus to the Team Leader’s Zone of Control.
  • If the user is the Lieutenant of the Army, is on the game table, and not in a Null State (Unconscious, Dead, Sepsitorized…), the following effects are applied:
    • All Troopers in the Army List that provide Orders:
      • Possess the Regular Training Characteristic.
      • Have the Courage Special Skill. Troopers with the Religious Troop Special Skill don’t apply this Effect.
    • You may declare a Coordinated Order that includes the user by spending only the Special Lieutenant Order, without expenditure of Command Tokens or extra Orders.
    • The army is not affected by the Retreat! rule, and never enters a Retreat! situation.
  • If the user becomes the Lieutenant of the Army (for example via the Chain of Command Special Skill or after a Loss of Lieutenant situation), the previous Effects of Inspiring Leadership apply immediately after the user has been appointed as Lieutenant.

INSPIRING LEADERSHIP
AUTOMATIC SKILL
Obligatory
REQUIREMENTS
  • In order to apply the effects of Inspiring Leadership, the user must be the Lieutenant of the Army, be on the game table, and not be in a Null State (Unconscious, Dead, Sepsitorized...).
EFFECTS
  • All Troopers in the Army List that provide Orders:
    • Possess the Regular Training Characteristic.
    • Have the Courage Special Skill. Troopers with the Religious Troop Special Skill don't apply this Effect.
  • You may declare a Coordinated Order that includes the user by spending only the Special Lieutenant Order, without expenditure of Command Tokens or extra Orders.
  • If the user belongs to a Fireteam where all members already have the Regular Training Characteristic in their Unit Profiles, apply a +2 inch bonus to the Team Leader's Zone of Control when making Coherency Checks for the Fireteam.
  • The army is not affected by the Retreat! rule, and never enters a Retreat! situation.
