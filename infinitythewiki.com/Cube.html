This indicates that the Trooper has an automated memory recording and storage device, commonly known as a Cube.

Troopers with a Cube are easier to heal. Additionally, Cube-wearing casualties can potentially be recovered for use in future missions.

Update PDF 5.2, October 2025
There are two different types of Cubes.

CUBE
AUTOMATIC EQUIPMENT
EFFECTS
  • When using the Doctor Special Skill (or other Special Skills or pieces of Equipment that explicitly say so) to heal a Trooper with a Cube, you can expend Command Tokens to re-roll a failed WIP Roll.


CUBE 2.0
AUTOMATIC EQUIPMENT
EFFECTS
  • When using the Doctor Special Skill (or other Special Skills or pieces of Equipment that explicitly say so) to heal a Trooper with a Cube 2.0, you can expend Command Tokens to re-roll a failed WIP Roll.
  • The user applies a +2 MOD to their Saving Roll when making a Saving Roll against an Attack with Sepsitor or Sepsitor Plus.
