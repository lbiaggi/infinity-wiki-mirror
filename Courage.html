This trooper is unafraid of death and ready to lay down his life in the execution of his duty.

COURAGE
AUTOMATIC SKILL
Optional
EFFECTS
  • Courage allows its user to choose to automatically pass any Guts Rolls.
  • Troopers with the Courage Special Skill are unaffected by Retreat! situations, so they cannot enter Retreat! State and act normally until the end of the game.
  • However, even in a Retreat! situation, Troops possessing Courage are affected by Loss of Lieutenant.
