This mobile weapons platform has been designed to be deployed on the battlefield at a distance, acting as a Mine with E/M Special Ammunition.

WILDPARROT
EFFECTS
  • These weapons are placed with the Place Deployable or Intuitive Attack Common Skills, always applying the Deployable and Perimeter rule.
  • Therefore, when players deploy this weapon, they place the WildParrot totally inside the Zone of Control of the Trooper, instead of placing it in Silhouette contact.
  • Deployed WildParrots work like E/M Mines, except that a WildParrot Token or Model is placed instead of a Camouflage Marker.


SEE ALSO

Once deployed, WildParrots work like E/M Mines.
